Advice from people who invest their own money too.

Seven services for three kinds of client. Each engagement is led personally by a principal who has raised and closed deals with his own money at stake.

Who we work with

Three kinds of client

Family offices and principals

Families that want direct deals and co-investments, not products. We bring origination, structure and principal-side judgement, and we are discreet by habit, not by policy.

Founders and management teams

Businesses raising from family offices, or building through acquisition. We know what investors want to see before a first meeting.

Platforms and sponsors

Buy-and-build vehicles and their backers. We design the roll-up, negotiate the term sheet and run the investor side so the operators can run the business.

How an engagement runs

Four simple steps

01

First call

Thirty minutes, no deck. We tell you honestly whether we can help, and who else might.

02

Scoping note

Two pages: the question, what we will deliver, who does the work, what it costs. Yours to keep either way.

03

The work

Weekly check-ins, one point of contact, decisions documented. We work alongside your existing advisers.

04

Afterwards

A closing memo and, where it makes sense, a light retainer so the relationships we opened stay warm.

What we do not do

Clear lines keep good relationships

  • We do not give regulated investment advice, manage third-party assets or run a fund for outside investors.
  • We do not work with retail investors, and we do not run mass fundraising campaigns.
  • We do not build the financial model for you. We work with your analysts, or help you hire good ones.
  • We do not take more than a handful of mandates at a time, so that each gets a principal, not a team of juniors.
