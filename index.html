Investor and founder

Guy delle Piane runs Snowball, an operating and investment holding. He has spent fifteen years on private deals, from M&A at a European investment bank to co-founding two buy-and-build platforms, and has founded companies himself, selling one in 2022. He has lived in Germany, France, Spain, the US, India, Italy and the UK.

Having sat on both sides, he understands what investors look for and what founders go through.
